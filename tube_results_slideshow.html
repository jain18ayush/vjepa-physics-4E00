Tube exploration · direction only
Property: direction (angle)
Scope

The tube is for direction — not speed or acceleration

Every tube experiment in this project sits on the Part 2 periodic direction spline at layer 5 (train / velocity clips). Speed and acceleration were already flat: spline ≈ chord, so there was nothing tube-like to unpack.

Direction

Circular spine through 64 angle centroids. Thick residual around it. This is the tube.

Speed

Low-dim, almost linear. Chord matches spline. No tube campaign.

Acceleration

Even flatter (~2 nullspace dims). Same story: no tube campaign.

Property under study: heading angle θ of the blue disk, encoded in the mean-pooled residual at L* = 5.

Setup

What was already true before the tube work

  • Part 1: direction needs ~41 orthogonal probes (dim ~82); multi-probe steering toward a fixed angle works.
  • Part 2: a periodic spline through angle centroids walks intermediate angles (~5° mid-path error); a chord teleports (~171°).
  • But the spline’s endpoint still loses to multi-probe (~18° vs ~8° on 0°→180°).

Hypothesis: those extra Part 1 dimensions are not 82 different concepts. They are thickness around a 1D circle — a tube.

Object of study for the rest of this deck.

Result 6 · copies

Part 1 probes sit in the tube — not on top variance axes

  • Early nullspace probe planes overlap the 20-D tube subspace a lot (~0.71–0.89 for the first five).
  • But variance-ordered tube prefixes capture almost none of that probe energy early: mean overlap sq. = 0.003 / 0.007 / 0.05 at k = 1 / 2 / 5, only 0.45 at k = 20.

So “multi-probe directions” and “leading residual PCs” are different slices of the same fat neighborhood. That is why resid-PCA k=2 and nullspace-tube k=1 both steered well — different rulers, same cloud.

Leading variance is where speed sits. Steering-relevant axes are not the same as “biggest leftover variance.”

0.003 → 0.45probe overlap energy as tube k grows 1 → 20

Hypothesis C score: partial. Probes ⊂ tube neighborhood, but ≠ top PCs.

Takeaway

Practical recipe on this data

  • Walk the periodic spline when you care about intermediate angles.
  • Add ~2 normal coordinates (residual PCA or first nullspace directions, orthogonal to the tangent) to nail a hard endpoint.
  • That unifies Part 1’s multi-probe hammer with Part 2’s manifold walk.
  • Do not treat direction and speed as independent 1D splines without residualizing — speed is entangled in the normal bundle.
